T1 · Alignment

RSSI to Voltage

Turns the RSSI voltage on your multimeter into received signal level in dBm, or the target level back into the voltage to aim for, using the Huawei calibration curve. (opens in new tab)

V = (RSL + 91.6) ÷ 15.9

 T2 · Planning

Link Budget

Receive level and fade margin for a microwave hop, in clear sky and in rain. Takes transmit power, frequency, distance, both antenna diameters and losses, with Malaysian rain zones and ITU-R P.838-3 and P.530-17 rain attenuation. (opens in new tab)

RSL = Ptx + Ga + Gb − FSPL − losses

 T3 · Planning

FSPL Calculator

Free-space path loss from frequency and distance, in MHz or GHz and metres or kilometres, with the working shown term by term and the wavelength. (opens in new tab)

FSPL = 20log₁₀(f) + 20log₁₀(d) + 32.44

02 About

A field engineer who also writes the tools

I have spent 9 years installing and configuring microwave transmission links, working since 2017 as a Microwave Telecommunication Engineer with MTK Communication.

Most of that work is on Huawei equipment: microwave transmission, RAN, TI NodeB and eNodeB. I also set up, configure and troubleshoot NEC microwave radios, and I lead a team on site as team leader and tester.

Away from the tower I write software. I work with Python, HTML and CSS, Java and Linux, and I follow digital communication, network and cyber security, automation and artificial intelligence. The calculators on this site come from that side of my work.
